Privacy, and how we contact businesses

Last updated: November 2026

Who we are

Larkspan is a small web and IT studio based in London. You can reach us at hello@larkspan.co.uk.

What information we use

  • Business details that are already public: your business name, type, address, phone number and business email address, as published on OpenStreetMap.
  • Your company record from Companies House, to confirm you are a registered limited company or LLP.
  • Any emails you send us, and our replies.

Why we contact you

We offer websites and IT services to small businesses. We only send unsolicited emails to registered companies and LLPs, at business addresses, and every email says who we are and how to stop hearing from us. We rely on legitimate interests: offering a relevant business service to a business. We don't email sole traders or partnerships unless they've asked us to.

Demo websites

Our demo sites use only public details about your business. Each one is clearly marked as a demo, is hidden from search engines and is not your official website. Ask us and we'll delete yours straight away.

How we use AI

We use AI tools to draft demo sites and to help write and answer emails. If you ask whether you're talking to a person or our AI assistant, we'll always tell you.

Who else processes the data

Our email provider (Zoho Mail), code and website hosting (GitHub), and our AI provider (Groq). We don't sell or share your details with anyone else.

How long we keep it

If you don't reply, we delete your details within 12 months. If you ask us to stop, we keep only your email address on a do-not-contact list, so we never email you again.

Your rights

You can ask us what we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, or object to being contacted. Email us and we'll act within one month, usually much sooner. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.
